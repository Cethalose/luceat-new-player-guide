# Luceat New Player's Guide Notices

This guide describes the currently supported character-choice surface in Luceat's `dnd-5e-srd-5.1` ruleset as of September 7, 2026.

The Luceat ruleset incorporates mechanics and names from the Dungeons & Dragons 5.1 System Reference Document where entries are marked `srd-incorporated` in Luceat's internal accounting.
