# Artificer Mechanical Progression

This original Luceat guide explains the Board-supplied intended mechanics first. Every feature states its separate implementation boundary. **Not verified** means the mechanic belongs to progression but should not be assumed usable in Luceat today. The accepted 2014 PHB guide remains preserved; other non-PHB paths are deliberately deferred.

## Artificer

- **Artificer L1 — Magical Tinkering and Spellcasting.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Maintain minor effects on objects up to Intelligence modifier (minimum one). Prepare Intelligence modifier + half Artificer level rounded down spells (minimum one) after a long rest. Intelligence controls spells; tools or an infused item can be the focus. Know two cantrips and have two 1st-rank slots.
- **Artificer L2 — Infuse Item.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Learn four designs and maintain two different infusions on eligible nonmagical objects after a long rest. Exceeding the limit ends the oldest; exchange one known design when gaining a level.
- **Artificer L3 — Specialist and Right Tool.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Choose one specialist, gaining features at 3, 5, 9, and 15. Spend an uninterrupted hour using tools to create one artisan-tool set. Extra Attack is not universal.
- **Artificer L4 — Ability improvement.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Increase one ability by 2 or two by 1 within the cap, or take an allowed feat.
- **Artificer L5 — Specialist feature and 2nd-rank spells.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain only the specialist L5 feature; slots become 4/2. No universal Extra Attack.
- **Artificer L6 — Tool Expertise and infusion growth.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Double proficiency on proficient tool checks; know six infusions, maintain three, unlock L6 prerequisites.
- **Artificer L7 — Flash of Genius.** *Luceat support: not verified.* After a visible creature within 30 feet rolls a check/save but before resolution, react to add Intelligence. Uses equal Intelligence modifier (minimum one) per long rest.
- **Artificer L8 — Ability improvement.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Improve abilities or take an allowed feat.
- **Artificer L9 — Specialist feature and 3rd-rank spells.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain the L9 specialist feature; slots become 4/3/2.
- **Artificer L10 — Magic Item Adept.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Attune four items; common/uncommon crafting takes quarter time and half gold. Know three cantrips/eight infusions, maintain four, unlock L10 prerequisites.
- **Artificer L11 — Spell-Storing Item.** *Luceat support: not verified.* After a long rest store an action-cast 1st/2nd-rank Artificer spell in a weapon/focus. A holder uses an action and your modifier, concentrating if needed. Uses equal twice Intelligence (minimum twice); replacement empties it.
- **Artificer L12 — Ability improvement.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Improve abilities or take a feat.
- **Artificer L13 — 4th-rank spells.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Slots become 4/3/3/1.
- **Artificer L14 — Magic Item Savant.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Attune five items and ignore class, race, spell, and level item requirements. Know ten infusions, maintain five, unlock L14 prerequisites.
- **Artificer L15 — Specialist feature.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain the specialist final feature; no universal Extra Attack.
- **Artificer L16 — Ability improvement.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Improve abilities or take a feat.
- **Artificer L17 — 5th-rank spells.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Slots become 4/3/3/3/1.
- **Artificer L18 — Magic Item Master.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Attune six items; know twelve infusions and maintain six.
- **Artificer L19 — Ability improvement.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Improve abilities or take a feat; slots become 4/3/3/3/2.
- **Artificer L20 — Soul of Artifice.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Add +1 to every save per attuned item. If damage would reduce you to 0 without killing outright, react to end one infusion and remain at 1 hit point.

### Spellcasting, infusions, and prerequisites

Slot breakpoints: L1 `2`; L3 `3`; L5 `4/2`; L7 `4/3`; L9 `4/3/2`; L12 `4/3/3`; L13 `4/3/3/1`; L14 `4/3/3/2`; L17 `4/3/3/3/1`; L19 `4/3/3/3/2`. Specialist spells are always prepared and do not count against the total.

*Luceat support: not verified.* Infusions include Enhanced Focus, Defense, and Weapon (+1, becoming +2 at L10); Returning Weapon; Repeating Shot; Strength Armor; Mind Sharpener; Homunculus Servant; L6 Winding Boots, Radiant Weapon, Repulsion Shield, Resistant Armor, and Spell-Refueling Ring; L10 Awareness Helm; L14 Propulsion Armor; and Replicate Item. Replication opens common utility items at L2, further uncommon choices at L6/L10, and selected rare choices at L14. Each item is a separate learned infusion, keeps attunement requirements, and must use an eligible object.

## Field Alchemist

- **Field Alchemist L3 — Experimental Elixir.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain alchemist supplies and specialist spells at levels 3/5/9/13/17. After a long rest make one random elixir, two at L6, three at L15. An action drinks/administers it. Effects: 2d4 + Intelligence healing; +10 speed one hour; +1 AC ten minutes; +1d4 attacks/saves one minute; fly 10 for ten minutes; or alter-self change ten minutes. Spend a slot to choose the result.
- **Field Alchemist L5 — Alchemical Savant.** *Luceat support: not verified.* When supplies are the focus, add Intelligence once per spell to healing or one acid, fire, necrotic, or poison damage roll.
- **Field Alchemist L9 — Restorative Reagents.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Elixirs grant 2d6 + Intelligence temporary HP; cast lesser restoration without slot/preparation Intelligence times per long rest.
- **Field Alchemist L15 — Chemical Mastery.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain acid/poison resistance and poisoned immunity; cast greater restoration and heal once each per long rest without slot or material.

## Warding Smith

- **Warding Smith L3 — Arcane Armor.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain smith tools, heavy armor, and specialist spells. Armor ignores Strength, acts as focus, resists removal, and replaces limbs. Guardian has Intelligence 1d8 gauntlets that hinder attacks on others and bonus-action level-sized temporary HP proficiency times/long rest. Infiltrator has Intelligence 1d6 launcher plus 1d6 once/turn, +5 speed, and improved Stealth.
- **Warding Smith L5 — Extra Attack.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Attack twice with the Attack action. This is specialist-only.
- **Warding Smith L9 — Armor Modifications.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Chest, boots, helmet, and special weapon are separate infusion targets; maintain two extra armor-only infusions.
- **Warding Smith L15 — Perfected Armor.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Guardian reacts proficiency times/long rest to pull and possibly strike a nearby creature. Infiltrator launcher hits illuminate, grant advantage on the next attack, and add 1d6 lightning if it hits.

## Clockwork Sapper

- **Clockwork Sapper L3 — Eldritch Cannon.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain woodcarver tools and specialist spells. Action-create one hour-long Tiny/Small AC18 cannon with HP five times level, free once/long rest or for a slot. Bonus-action command within 60 feet: 15-foot cone 2d8 fire (Dexterity half), 120-foot attack 2d8 force plus push, or 10-foot aura granting 1d8 + Intelligence temporary HP.
- **Clockwork Sapper L5 — Arcane Firearm.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Make one rod/staff/wand a focus adding 1d8 to one Artificer-spell damage roll.
- **Clockwork Sapper L9 — Explosive Cannon.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Cannon damage rises 1d8; action-detonate within 60 feet for 3d8 force in 20 feet, Dexterity half.
- **Clockwork Sapper L15 — Fortified Position.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Cannons grant half cover within 10 feet; create two different-mode cannons and activate both with one bonus action.

## Steelbound Companion Smith

- **Steelbound Companion Smith L3 — Battle Ready and Steel Defender.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Gain smith tools, martial weapons, specialist spells, and Intelligence attacks with an attuned magic weapon. Defender: AC15, HP 2 + Intelligence + five times level, speed 40, scaling saves/skills, poison immunity, 1d8 + proficiency force strike, and defensive reaction. It dodges unless bonus-action commanded; mending heals 2d6; rebuilding costs one hour and a slot.
- **Steelbound Companion Smith L5 — Extra Attack.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Attack twice with the Attack action. This is specialist-only.
- **Steelbound Companion Smith L9 — Arcane Jolt.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Once/turn after your magic weapon or defender hits, add 2d6 force or heal a visible creature/object within 30 feet for 2d6. Intelligence uses per long rest.
- **Steelbound Companion Smith L15 — Improved Defender.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Jolt becomes 4d6; defender gains +2 AC and its defensive reaction deals 1d4 + Intelligence force if the attack still hits.

## Specialist spell lists

*Luceat support: not verified.* Field Alchemist: healing word/ray of sickness; flaming sphere/acid arrow; gaseous form/mass healing word; blight/death ward; cloudkill/raise dead. Warding Smith: magic missile/thunderwave; mirror image/shatter; hypnotic pattern/lightning bolt; fire shield/greater invisibility; passwall/wall of force. Clockwork Sapper: shield/thunderwave; scorching ray/shatter; fireball/wind wall; ice storm/wall of fire; cone of cold/wall of force. Steelbound Companion Smith: heroism/shield; branding smite/warding bond; aura of vitality/conjure barrage; aura of purity/fire shield; banishing smite/mass cure wounds. Each pair arrives at specialist levels 3, 5, 9, 13, and 17 respectively.

## Implementation boundary

These intended mechanics remain defined where Luceat cannot execute them. The adjacent status does not imply working persistence, targeting, companions, commands, recovery, spell choice, or resolution. Consult the main guide for observed support.
