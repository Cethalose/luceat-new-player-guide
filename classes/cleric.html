Shared spellcasting progression

Spellcasting slots recover on LR. Cantrips do not use slots. A spell must be known or prepared as required and cast using a slot at least equal to its level. Higher-level slots do not themselves authorize learning higher-level spells. Ritual casting adds 10 minutes, uses no slot, and cannot raise the spell’s level.

Domain/oath/land spells are always prepared, count as spells of the granting class, and do not reduce preparation capacity. Patron spells only expand the warlock’s choices; they are not automatically known. Magical Secrets spells count as bard spells. “Know” and “prepare” are not interchangeable states.

Fighter/Rogue one-third spellcasting

Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster start at L3. Both use INT and wizard spells. EK knows two wizard cantrips at L3 and three at L10; AT knows mage hand plus two other wizard cantrips at L3, plus one more at L10. Cantrips are separate from leveled spells known. At L3 each knows three L1 spells: two school-restricted and one unrestricted. EK restricted schools are abjuration/evocation; AT restricted schools are enchantment/illusion. Newly learned leveled spells are school-restricted except the spells learned at L8, L14, and L20. Each class level may replace one known spell with an eligible wizard spell; replacement preserves the school restriction except when replacing an unrestricted spell from L3/8/14/20. Neither subclass grants ritual casting or use of an arcane focus.

Shared fighting styles

Choose only from the class’s list: Fighter = all six; Paladin = Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection; Ranger = Archery, Defense, Dueling, Two-Weapon Fighting. The same style cannot be selected twice even if a feature grants another choice.

StyleMechanical effect
Archery+2 to attack rolls made with ranged weapons.
Defense+1 AC while wearing armor.
Dueling+2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon wielded in one hand while wielding no other weapon. A shield is not a weapon merely because it is held.
Great Weapon FightingWhen a damage die for an attack with a melee weapon wielded in two hands rolls 1 or 2, reroll that die and use the new result. Weapon must have two-handed or versatile property. Treat the weapon’s own damage dice as the affected dice; preserve an interpretation flag if the implementation uses a different adjudication.
ProtectionWhen a creature you can see attacks a target other than you within 5 ft of you, use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll. Requires wielding a shield.
Two-Weapon FightingAdd the relevant ability modifier to damage of the second attack granted by two-weapon fighting. It does not waive weapon eligibility or bonus-action cost.

Channel Divinity (L2). Luceat support: not verified. Shared pool for Turn Undead and domain options; one use, two at L6, three at L18. SR/LR recovery. DC = cleric spell DC. Turn Undead: action, present holy symbol; every undead within 30 ft that can see or hear you makes WIS save. Failure: turned 1 minute or until damaged. Turned creature spends turns moving away, cannot willingly approach within 30 ft, and has no reactions; action restricted to Dash or escaping restraint preventing movement. If nowhere to move, may Dodge. Destroy Undead (L5). Luceat support: not verified. A turned undead failing this save is destroyed if CR ≤ 1/2; threshold 1 at L8, 2 at L11, 3 at L14, 4 at L17.

Divine Intervention (L10). Luceat support: not verified. Action to request help and roll percentile. Success if result ≤ L; DM determines intervention, with a cleric/domain spell effect an appropriate model. On success, unavailable for 7 days; on failure, available after LR. L20 succeeds automatically; 7-day cooldown remains. Do not automatically grant a selectable wish-like spell menu.

Domain weapon/cantrip progression. Luceat support: not verified. Divine Strike (Life, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War): once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, add 1d8 of domain’s type, 2d8 at cleric L14. Potent Spellcasting (Knowledge, Light): add WIS to damage dealt by a cleric cantrip.

Lantern Archive Domain

  • L1 Blessings of Knowledge: Luceat support: not verified. two languages; proficiency in two of Arcana, History, Nature, Religion and double PB on checks using the chosen skills.
  • L2 Knowledge of the Ages: Luceat support: not verified. action, spend Channel Divinity; gain proficiency in one skill or tool for 10 minutes.
  • L6 Read Thoughts: Luceat support: not verified. action, spend Channel Divinity; one creature visible within 60 ft makes WIS save. Success prevents using this feature on it until LR. Failure permits reading surface thoughts while within 60 ft for 1 minute. During that time, action to end the effect and cast suggestion on it without a slot; it automatically fails that spell’s save. Retain the spell’s remaining conditions.
  • L8 Potent Spellcasting: Luceat support: not verified. as above.
  • L17 Visions of the Past: Luceat support: not verified. meditate up to WIS minutes, minimum 1, concentrating as on a spell. Once per SR/LR. Object mode: hold object; after 1 minute learn last owner’s appearance, how they obtained/lost it, and their most recent significant associated event; another minute per earlier owner who possessed it during the preceding number of days equal to your Wisdom score. Area mode: see significant local events within up to a 50-ft cube, most recent first, one day per minute, looking back up to your Wisdom score in days.

Life Domain

  • L1: Luceat support: not verified. heavy armor proficiency; Disciple of Life adds 2 + spell level to HP restored when using a spell of L1+ to restore HP.
  • L2 Preserve Life: Luceat support: not verified. action, spend Channel Divinity; allocate 5 × cleric L healing among creatures within 30 ft, including yourself. Cannot raise a target above half its HP maximum; cannot heal constructs or undead.
  • L6 Blessed Healer: Luceat support: not verified. when casting a L1+ spell restoring HP to a creature other than yourself, regain 2 + spell level HP yourself.
  • L8 Divine Strike: Luceat support: not verified. radiant.
  • L17 Supreme Healing: Luceat support: not verified. replace each die rolled to restore HP with a spell with its maximum result; no maximization of non-spell healing.

Dawn Vigil Domain / Ember Mercy Domain

  • L1: Luceat support: not verified. learn light as an additional cleric cantrip. Warding Flare: reaction when attacked by a visible creature within 30 ft; impose disadvantage before attack resolves. Attacker immune if it cannot be blinded. max(1, WIS) uses/LR.
  • L2 Radiance of the Dawn: Luceat support: not verified. action, spend Channel Divinity and present holy symbol. Dispel magical darkness within 30 ft; hostile creatures within 30 ft make CON save or take 2d10 + L radiant damage, half on success. Total cover protects from this feature.
  • L6 Improved Flare: Luceat support: not verified. Warding Flare can protect a creature other than you when an eligible attacker within 30 ft attacks it.
  • L8 Potent Spellcasting: Luceat support: not verified. as above.
  • L17 Corona of Light: Luceat support: not verified. action; bright light 60 ft and dim light another 30 ft, for 1 minute; dismiss with another action. Enemies in bright light have disadvantage on saves against spells dealing fire or radiant damage.
