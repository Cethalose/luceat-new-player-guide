Ability Score Improvement. At each listed milestone, increase one ability by 2 or two abilities by 1, without raising a score above 20; if feats are permitted, an eligible feat may replace the improvement.

Subclass feature. Choose or advance one of the Druid paths listed later on this page, using its feature at the listed level.

Druidic (L1). Luceat support: not verified. Know secret language; leave hidden messages. Other Druidic speakers automatically notice; non-speakers notice with DC 15 WIS (Perception) but cannot decipher without magic.

Wild Shape (L2). Luceat support: not verified. Action; become a beast you have seen. Two uses/SR or LR. Duration floor(L/2) hours per use; spend another use to continue, or revert automatically when duration expires. Revert early with bonus action; automatically revert on unconsciousness, 0 HP, or death. Base maximum CR 1/4 and neither swim nor fly speed at L2; CR 1/2 and no fly speed at L4; CR 1 with flight allowed at L8. CR, movement gates, and seen-before eligibility are separate restrictions.

Use beast statistics except keep alignment/personality, INT/WIS/CHA, skill/save proficiencies; add beast skill/save proficiencies and use the higher bonus if both possess a proficiency. Cannot use a beast’s legendary or lair actions. Acquire its HP/Hit Dice; reverting restores prior form’s pre-transformation HP. If reverting because of 0 HP, excess damage transfers to normal form and only makes it unconscious if it also reaches 0. Cannot cast spells normally; speech/actions limited by form anatomy. Transformation does not break concentration and permits actions of spells already cast. Retain class/race/other features that new anatomy can use, but special senses require the form to possess that sense. Choose equipment to drop, merge, or be worn if practical. Merged equipment has no effect; equipment does not resize automatically.

Timeless Body (L18). Luceat support: not verified. Age 1 year per 10 elapsed years. Beast Spells (L18). Luceat support: not verified. In Wild Shape, can cast druid spells with verbal/somatic components using the form; still cannot provide material components. Archdruid (L20). Luceat support: not verified. Unlimited Wild Shape uses. For druid spells in any form ignore verbal/somatic components and materials that have no cost and are not consumed. Costly or consumed components remain required.

Circle of the Land

  • L2: Luceat support: not verified. one additional druid cantrip. Natural Recovery: during SR recover slots of total level ≤ ceil(L/2), none of L6+. Once, reset by LR.
  • L3 Circle Spells: Luceat support: not verified. choose one terrain, giving spells at L3/5/7/9 from table below. Always prepared; no preparation-capacity cost.
  • L6 Land’s Stride: Luceat support: not verified. ignore nonmagical difficult-terrain movement costs. Nonmagical plants neither slow nor damage you. Advantage on saves against magically manipulated/created plants that impede movement.
  • L10 Nature’s Ward: Luceat support: not verified. cannot be charmed/frightened by elementals/fey; immune to poison and disease.
  • L14 Nature’s Sanctuary: Luceat support: not verified. beast or plant attacking you makes WIS save against druid DC. Failure requires different target or automatic miss. Success grants immunity to this effect for 24 hours. Attacker is aware of this effect before attacking. Not applicable to every creature type.

Circle of Moonlit Forms

  • L2 Combat Wild Shape: Luceat support: not verified. may enter Wild Shape with bonus action instead of action. While transformed, bonus action to expend spell slot and heal 1d8 per slot level.
  • L2 Circle Forms: Luceat support: not verified. maximum beast CR 1 immediately, increasing to floor(L/3) at L6. Other Wild Shape restrictions, including swim/fly gates and seen-before requirement, remain.
  • L6 Primal Strike: Luceat support: not verified. beast-form attacks count as magical against resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks/damage.
  • L10 Elemental Wild Shape: Luceat support: not verified. expend two uses at once to become air, earth, fire, or water elemental. Other Wild Shape rules apply except changes required by elemental form eligibility.
  • L14 Thousand Forms: Luceat support: not verified. cast alter self at will; normal concentration and spell rules apply.
