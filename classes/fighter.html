Arcane Weapon Adept spellcasting progression

This Fighter subclass begins spellcasting at level 3 using Intelligence and wizard spells. Most learned spells come from abjuration and evocation; the choices gained at levels 3, 8, 14, and 20 may ignore that school restriction. It does not gain ritual casting or an arcane focus.

Fighter Fighting Styles

Choose one style from this Fighter list when the class progression grants Fighting Style. The same style cannot be selected twice.

StyleMechanical effect
