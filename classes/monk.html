Unarmored Defense (L1): Luceat support: not verified. no armor/shield, AC 10 + DEX + WIS. Martial Arts (L1): Luceat support: not verified. while unarmored, without shield, and unarmed or wielding only monk weapons, may use DEX instead of STR for attack/damage with unarmed strikes and monk weapons; may replace their normal damage die with Martial Arts die. Monk weapons are shortswords and simple melee weapons without two-handed or heavy property. After taking Attack action with unarmed strike/monk weapon on your turn, may make one unarmed strike as bonus action. Die d4, d6 at L5, d8 at L11, d10 at L17.

Ki (L2): Luceat support: not verified. pool = L; SR/LR recovery requires at least 30 minutes of the rest in meditation. Ki DC 8 + PB + WIS. Flurry of Blows: immediately after Attack action on your turn, spend 1 ki for two unarmed strikes as bonus action. Patient Defense: 1 ki for Dodge as bonus action. Step of the Wind: 1 ki for Disengage or Dash as bonus action; jump distance doubled that turn.

Unarmored Movement (L2): Luceat support: not verified. without armor/shield add 10 ft speed, then 15 L6, 20 L10, 25 L14, 30 L18. At L9 can run along vertical surfaces/across liquids during own turn without falling during movement; cannot remain unsupported after move. Deflect Missiles (L3): Luceat support: not verified. reaction when hit by ranged weapon attack; reduce damage by 1d10 + DEX + L. If reduced to zero, may catch missile if small enough for one hand and a hand is free. On catching, may spend 1 ki within same reaction to make ranged attack with missile, range 20/60 ft, proficient; treat it as monk weapon. Slow Fall (L4): Luceat support: not verified. reaction when falling, reduce fall damage by 5 × L.

Extra Attack (L5): Luceat support: not verified. two attacks. Stunning Strike (L5): Luceat support: not verified. on melee weapon attack hit, spend 1 ki; target CON save or stunned until end of your next turn. Unarmed strikes qualify as melee weapon attacks. Ki-Empowered Strikes (L6): Luceat support: not verified. unarmed attacks count as magical against resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks/damage. Evasion (L7): Luceat support: not verified. against an effect offering a DEX save for half damage, successful save takes none; failed save takes half. Stillness of Mind (L7): Luceat support: not verified. action to end one effect causing you to be charmed or frightened; does not authorize an action prohibited by that effect.

Purity of Body (L10): Luceat support: not verified. immune to disease and poison. Tongue of the Sun and Moon (L13): Luceat support: not verified. understand all spoken languages; any creature understanding a language understands your speech. Diamond Soul (L14): Luceat support: not verified. proficiency in all saving throws, including death saves; on failed save spend 1 ki to reroll, must use new result. Timeless Body (L15): Luceat support: not verified. no frailty from age, cannot be magically aged, still die of old age; no food/water requirement. Empty Body (L18): Luceat support: not verified. action, 4 ki, invisible 1 minute and resistant to all damage except force. Alternatively 8 ki to cast astral projection without material components, only yourself. Perfect Self (L20): Luceat support: not verified. initiative with no ki restores 4.

Way of the Open Hand

  • L3 Open Hand Technique: Luceat support: not verified. on a Flurry of Blows hit choose one: DEX save or prone; STR save or push up to 15 ft; or no reactions until end of your next turn, without a save.
  • L6 Wholeness of Body: Luceat support: not verified. action to heal 3 × L; once/LR.
  • L11 Tranquility: Luceat support: not verified. on finishing LR gain sanctuary until start of next LR or until spell ends normally. Save DC = ki DC.
  • L17 Quivering Palm: Luceat support: not verified. after unarmed strike hits creature, spend 3 ki to establish vibrations lasting L days. Only one creature at a time; may end harmlessly without action. While on same plane, action to end vibrations harmfully: target CON save; failure 0 HP, success 10d10 necrotic damage. Not a spell and not an automatic death effect.

Way of the Veiled Step

  • L3 Shadow Arts: Luceat support: not verified. learn minor illusion. Spend 2 ki to cast darkness, darkvision, pass without trace, or silence, no material components. Retain normal casting times/concentration.
  • L6 Shadow Step: Luceat support: not verified. while in dim light/darkness, bonus action teleport up to 60 ft to visible unoccupied space also in dim light/darkness. Advantage on first melee attack before end of turn.
  • L11 Cloak of Shadows: Luceat support: not verified. action in dim light/darkness to become invisible until making an attack, casting a spell, or entering bright light.
  • L17 Opportunist: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when creature within 5 ft is hit by another creature’s attack: make melee attack against that creature.

Way of the Still Flame

Disciple of the Elements (L3): Luceat support: not verified. know Elemental Attunement plus one other eligible discipline. Learn one additional discipline at L6, L11, L17. At those milestones may also replace one known discipline with another eligible discipline. Elemental Attunement can be the replaced discipline. Maximum total known remains 2/3/4/5. Discipline spellcasting requires no material components; use WIS/ki DC. Other spell requirements remain.

At L5 may add ki to increase the level of a discipline spell that gains an enhanced effect at higher level: +1 spell level per additional ki. Maximum ki per casting, including base cost: 3 at L5–8, 4 at L9–12, 5 at L13–16, 6 at L17–20. This casting cap does not cap non-spell disciplines’ own extra-ki damage clauses. The prerequisite column means monk level.
