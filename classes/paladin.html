Paladin spellcasting

Spell slots recover on a long rest. Cantrips do not spend slots. A spell must be known or prepared as described below, and its slot must be at least the spell's rank.

Paladin Fighting Styles

Choose one style from this Paladin list when the class progression grants Fighting Style. The same style cannot be selected twice.

StyleMechanical effect
Defense+1 AC while wearing armor.
Dueling+2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon wielded in one hand while wielding no other weapon. A shield is not a weapon merely because it is held.
Great Weapon FightingWhen a damage die for an attack with a melee weapon wielded in two hands rolls 1 or 2, reroll that die and use the new result. Weapon must have two-handed or versatile property. Treat the weapon’s own damage dice as the affected dice; preserve an interpretation flag if the implementation uses a different adjudication.
ProtectionWhen a creature you can see attacks a target other than you within 5 ft of you, use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll. Requires wielding a shield.

Oath of Devotion

  • L3 Sacred Weapon: Luceat support: not verified. action, Channel Divinity, imbue held weapon for 1 minute: add max(1, CHA) to attack rolls, weapon counts magical, bright light 20 ft and dim another 20 ft. End as part of another action; ends if no longer holding/carrying weapon or unconscious.
  • L3 Turn the Unholy: Luceat support: not verified. action, Channel Divinity; each fiend/undead within 30 ft that can see/hear you makes WIS save, turned on failure for 1 minute or until damaged. Apply Turn Undead behavior described under cleric.
  • L7 Aura of Devotion: Luceat support: not verified. conscious you and friendly creatures within 10 ft cannot be charmed; 30 ft at L18.
  • L15 Purity of Spirit: Luceat support: not verified. continuously under protection from evil and good.
  • L20 Holy Nimbus: Luceat support: not verified. action, once/LR, 1 minute: bright light 30 ft, dim another 30; enemy starting turn in bright light takes 10 radiant. During effect, advantage on saves against spells cast by fiends/undead.

Oath of the Star Watch

  • L3 Nature’s Wrath: Luceat support: not verified. action, Channel Divinity; visible creature within 10 ft makes its choice of STR or DEX save or is restrained. Repeats same choice at end of each turn to end effect on success.
  • L3 Turn the Faithless: Luceat support: not verified. action, Channel Divinity; fey/fiends within 30 ft that can hear you make WIS saves, turned for 1 minute or until damaged using ordinary turning behavior. A turned creature whose real form is concealed by illusion, shapechanging, or another effect reveals that form.
  • L7 Aura of Warding: Luceat support: not verified. you/friendly creatures within 10 ft resist damage from spells; 30 ft at L18. This is not resistance to all magical damage.
  • L15 Undying Sentinel: Luceat support: not verified. when reduced to 0 HP without outright death, may remain at 1 HP, once/LR. No frailty from aging; cannot be magically aged.
  • L20 Elder Champion: Luceat support: not verified. action, once/LR, 1 minute: regain 10 HP at start of turns; paladin spells with 1-action casting time may use bonus action instead; enemies within 10 ft have disadvantage on saves against your paladin spells and Channel Divinity options.

Oath of the Open Road

  • L3 Abjure Enemy: Luceat support: not verified. action, Channel Divinity; visible creature within 60 ft makes WIS save unless immune to frightened; fiend/undead has disadvantage. Failure frightened for 1 minute or until damaged, speed 0 and no speed bonuses. Success halves speed for 1 minute or until damaged.
  • L3 Vow of Enmity: Luceat support: not verified. bonus action, Channel Divinity; visible creature within 10 ft; advantage on attacks against it for 1 minute or until it reaches 0 HP or falls unconscious.
  • L7 Relentless Avenger: Luceat support: not verified. when hitting with an opportunity attack, move up to half speed immediately as part of same reaction, without provoking opportunity attacks.
  • L15 Soul of Vengeance: Luceat support: not verified. when target of Vow makes an attack, reaction to make melee weapon attack against it if within reach.
  • L20 Avenging Angel: Luceat support: not verified. action, once/LR, 1 hour: flight 60 ft; 30-ft menace aura. First time an enemy enters aura or starts turn there during a battle, WIS save or frightened of you for 1 minute or until damaged. Attacks against that creature have advantage while it is frightened by this aura.

Oath transgression/consequences are DM adjudication, not an automatic subclass switch. The DMG Oathbreaker is excluded.
