Ability Score Improvement. At each listed milestone, increase one ability by 2 or two abilities by 1, without raising a score above 20; if feats are permitted, an eligible feat may replace the improvement.

Subclass feature. Choose or advance one of the Ranger paths listed later on this page, using its feature at the listed level.

Favored Enemy (L1/6/14): Luceat support: not verified. choose a creature type (aberrations, beasts, celestials, constructs, dragons, elementals, fey, fiends, giants, monstrosities, oozes, plants, undead) or two humanoid races. Advantage on WIS (Survival) to track them and INT checks to recall information about them. Learn one language spoken by chosen enemies if they speak any. Each milestone adds a selection and eligible language; this does not add damage.

Natural Explorer (L1/6/10): Luceat support: not verified. choose arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, or Underdark; additional terrain at L6/10. On INT/WIS checks related to favored terrain, double PB if using a proficient skill. While traveling there for at least 1 hour: difficult terrain does not slow group travel; cannot become lost except magically; remain alert even when foraging/navigating/tracking; traveling alone can be stealthy at normal pace; find twice normal food when foraging; tracking reveals exact number, sizes, and elapsed time since creatures passed.

Fighting Style/Spellcasting (L2): Luceat support: not verified. shared rules. Primeval Awareness (L3): Luceat support: not verified. action, expend ranger slot; for 1 minute per slot level sense whether aberrations/celestials/dragons/elementals/fey/fiends/undead are within 1 mile, or 6 miles in favored terrain. Does not reveal locations or numbers. Extra Attack (L5): Luceat support: not verified. two attacks. Land’s Stride (L8): Luceat support: not verified. same plant/nonmagical-terrain benefits as Land druid L6.

Hide in Plain Sight (L10): Luceat support: not verified. spend 1 minute camouflaging with naturally occurring materials. Can hide by pressing against solid surface at least as tall/wide as yourself; +10 DEX (Stealth) while remaining there without moving or taking actions. Moving, taking action, or reaction requires repeating camouflage to benefit again. Vanish (L14): Luceat support: not verified. Hide as bonus action; cannot be tracked nonmagically unless choosing to leave trail. Feral Senses (L18): Luceat support: not verified. inability to see a target does not impose disadvantage on your attacks. Know location of invisible creature within 30 ft if not hidden from you and you are neither blinded nor deafened. Foe Slayer (L20): Luceat support: not verified. once on each of your turns, add WIS to attack roll or damage roll against favored enemy; decide before/after roll but before effects applied.

Beast Bond Warden

L3 Ranger’s Companion: Luceat support: not verified. choose a beast of Medium size or smaller, CR ≤ 1/4. Apply PB to its AC, attack rolls, damage rolls, and proficient skill/save bonuses. HP maximum is greater of its normal maximum and 4 × ranger L. It can spend its Hit Dice on SR. It acts on your initiative. You may verbally direct movement without action; action to command Attack, Dash, Disengage, or Help. With Extra Attack, when using your action to command Attack, may also make one weapon attack yourself. Without a command it Dodges. Reactions do not require commands. If you are absent/incapacitated, it acts independently to protect itself/you. In favored terrain while traveling with only your beast, you can move stealthily at normal pace. If it dies, obtain a new companion by magically bonding for 8 hours with another eligible nonhostile beast.

L7 Exceptional Training: Luceat support: not verified. on a turn when companion does not attack, bonus action to command Dash/Disengage/Help. Companion attacks count as magical against resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks/damage. L11 Bestial Fury: Luceat support: not verified. when commanded to Attack, it makes two attacks or uses its Multiattack if available. L15 Share Spells: Luceat support: not verified. a spell you cast targeting yourself can also affect companion within 30 ft. Do not replace this companion with expansion Primal Companion stat blocks or their bonus-action Attack command.
