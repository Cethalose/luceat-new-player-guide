Ability Score Improvement. At each listed milestone, increase one ability by 2 or two abilities by 1, without raising a score above 20; if feats are permitted, an eligible feat may replace the improvement.

Subclass feature. Choose or advance one of the Rogue paths listed later on this page, using its feature at the listed level.

Expertise (L1/6): Luceat support: not verified. at each milestone choose two proficient skills or one proficient skill and thieves’ tools; double PB on chosen checks. Sneak Attack (L1): Luceat support: not verified. once per turn on a hit with finesse/ranged weapon, add ceil(L/2)d6 damage if you have advantage. Also eligible without advantage if another enemy of target is within 5 ft of it, that enemy is not incapacitated, and your roll lacks disadvantage. Apply ordinary advantage cancellation before eligibility. Thieves’ Cant (L1): Luceat support: not verified. encode messages in speech taking four times normal duration; only cant speakers understand; also understand secret signs/symbols conveying simple messages.

Cunning Action (L2): Luceat support: not verified. bonus action each of your turns for Dash/Disengage/Hide. Uncanny Dodge (L5): Luceat support: not verified. reaction when a visible attacker hits you; halve the attack’s damage. Evasion (L7): Luceat support: not verified. DEX save for half → none on success, half on failure. Reliable Talent (L11): Luceat support: not verified. proficient ability checks treat d20 results ≤9 as 10. Blindsense (L14): Luceat support: not verified. if able to hear, know location of hidden/invisible creatures within 10 ft; does not itself make them visible. Slippery Mind (L15): Luceat support: not verified. WIS save proficiency. Elusive (L18): Luceat support: not verified. no attack roll has advantage against you while you are not incapacitated. Stroke of Luck (L20): Luceat support: not verified. turn your missed attack against a target within range into a hit, or treat d20 of failed ability check as 20; once/SR or LR. Does not change a saving throw.

Thief

  • L3 Fast Hands: Luceat support: not verified. Cunning Action can make DEX (Sleight of Hand), use thieves’ tools to disarm trap/open lock, or take Use an Object action. Magic-item activation is not automatically Use an Object.
  • L3 Second-Story Work: Luceat support: not verified. climbing costs no extra movement; running jump distance +DEX feet.
  • L9 Supreme Sneak: Luceat support: not verified. advantage on DEX (Stealth) if moving no more than half speed that turn.
  • L13 Use Magic Device: Luceat support: not verified. ignore class, race, and level requirements on using magic items; does not waive attunement capacity or all other restrictions.
  • L17 Thief’s Reflexes: Luceat support: not verified. two turns in first combat round: normal initiative and initiative minus 10. Not usable when surprised. Each is a separate turn for Sneak Attack and reaction recovery.

Knife Dance Agent

  • L3 Bonus Proficiencies: Luceat support: not verified. disguise kit, poisoner’s kit.
  • L3 Assassinate: Luceat support: not verified. advantage against creatures that have not yet taken a turn in combat; any hit against surprised creature is critical. Winning initiative alone does not make a target surprised.
  • L9 Infiltration Expertise: Luceat support: not verified. spend 7 days and 25 gp establishing identity/history/profession/affiliations for yourself, not someone else’s identity. Other creatures accept disguise unless given obvious reason not to.
  • L13 Impostor: Luceat support: not verified. after at least 3 hours studying a person’s speech/handwriting/mannerisms, can mimic them; no suspicion to casual observer. If a wary observer suspects, advantage on CHA (Deception) to avoid detection.
  • L17 Death Strike: Luceat support: not verified. when you attack/hit surprised creature, CON save DC 8 + PB + DEX; failure doubles damage of attack. Combine with Assassinate’s critical dice before this damage doubling.

Spellhand Trickster

  • L3 Spellcasting: Luceat support: not verified. shared one-third table, mage hand and enchantment/illusion rules.
  • L3 Mage Hand Legerdemain: Luceat support: not verified. can make mage hand invisible; may stow held object in another creature’s worn/carried container, retrieve object from such container, or use thieves’ tools at range for locks/traps. DEX (Sleight of Hand) contested by WIS (Perception) to perform unnoticed. May control hand using Cunning Action bonus action. Spell’s ordinary range/weight/duration restrictions remain.
  • L9 Magical Ambush: Luceat support: not verified. if hidden from a creature when casting spell on it, it has disadvantage on saves against that spell this turn.
  • L13 Versatile Trickster: Luceat support: not verified. bonus action to designate creature within 5 ft of mage hand; advantage on attacks against it until end of turn.
  • L17 Spell Thief: Luceat support: not verified. reaction immediately after creature casts spell targeting you or including you in its area; caster INT save against your spell DC. Failure negates effect against you. If spell is at least L1 and of a level you can cast (not necessarily a wizard spell), steal knowledge: can cast using your slots for next 8 hours; original caster cannot cast it during that interval. Once/LR, whether save succeeds or fails.

Spellhand Trickster spellcasting progression

This Rogue subclass begins spellcasting at level 3 using Intelligence and wizard spells. Most learned spells come from enchantment and illusion; the choices gained at levels 3, 8, 14, and 20 may ignore that school restriction. It does not gain ritual casting or an arcane focus.
