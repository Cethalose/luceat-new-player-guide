PHB Metamagic: all eight

OptionPoint cost and mechanics
Careful Spell1: when casting spell forcing saves, choose up to max(1, CHA) creatures to automatically succeed on their saving throws against it. Does not grant immunity to half-on-success damage.
Distant Spell1: when casting spell with range at least 5 ft, double range; touch-range spell becomes 30 ft. Does not double a self-centered area’s radius.
Empowered Spell1: when rolling spell damage, reroll up to max(1, CHA) damage dice and use new results. May combine with another Metamagic on same casting.
Extended Spell1: spell duration of at least 1 minute doubles, maximum 24 hours. Does not remove concentration.
Heightened Spell3: when casting spell requiring save, one target has disadvantage on its first save against that spell.
Quickened Spell2: spell casting time of 1 action becomes bonus action for this casting. General bonus-action spell restrictions still apply.
Subtle Spell1: cast without verbal or somatic components. Material requirements remain.
Twinned SpellPoints equal spell level, or 1 for cantrip: a spell targeting only one creature, not range self, can target a second creature in range. Spell must be incapable of targeting more than one creature at its current level. Do not apply to area or multi-target-capable spells.

Draconic Bloodline

L1 Dragon Ancestor: Luceat support: not verified. choose black/copper → acid; blue/bronze → lightning; brass/gold/red → fire; green → poison; silver/white → cold. Speak/read/write Draconic. Double PB on CHA checks when interacting with dragons if PB applies. L1 Draconic Resilience: Luceat support: not verified. maximum HP +1 per sorcerer level, including first; without armor, AC 13 + DEX.

L6 Elemental Affinity: Luceat support: not verified. when casting spell dealing ancestry’s damage type, add CHA to one damage roll. At same time may spend 1 point for resistance to that damage type for 1 hour. L14 Dragon Wings: Luceat support: not verified. bonus action to manifest wings with fly speed equal to current speed, until dismissed with bonus action. Cannot manifest while wearing armor not accommodating them; non-accommodating clothing can be destroyed. L18 Draconic Presence: Luceat support: not verified. action, 5 points, concentrate up to 1 minute. Choose awe or fear; hostile creatures starting turn within 60 ft make WIS saves or become charmed (awe)/frightened (fear) until aura ends. Successful save grants immunity to this aura for 24 hours.

Wild Spark Bloodline

L1 Wild Magic Surge: Luceat support: not verified. once per turn, immediately after casting sorcerer spell of L1+, DM may request d20; 1 triggers d100 table below. Surge-generated spell cannot use Metamagic; if it ordinarily requires concentration, here it does not and lasts full duration. L1 Tides of Chaos: Luceat support: not verified. gain advantage on one attack, check, or save; once/LR. After use, DM may trigger an immediate surge after one of your L1+ sorcerer spells before the next LR; doing so restores Tides. Do not implement an automatic surge on every spell or an automatic Tides reset without that DM event.

L6 Bend Luck: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when visible creature makes attack/check/save; 2 points to roll d4 and add/subtract from its roll, after roll but before effects. L14 Controlled Chaos: Luceat support: not verified. roll twice on surge table, choose outcome. L18 Spell Bombardment: Luceat support: not verified. once per turn when rolling spell damage and any die has rolled its highest value, choose one such die, roll it again, and add the new result. Does not recursively trigger from additional die.
