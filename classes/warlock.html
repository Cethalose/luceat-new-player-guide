Eldritch Master (L20): Luceat support: not verified. spend 1 minute requesting patron’s aid to restore all expended Pact Magic slots, once/LR; no short rest required and no Arcanum recovery.

Pact Boons

Blade: Luceat support: not verified. action creates a melee weapon of chosen form in empty hand; proficient while wielding it; magical against resistance/immunity to nonmagical attacks/damage. Disappears when more than 5 ft away for at least 1 minute, when creating another, when dismissing without action, or on death. May bind one magic weapon with 1-hour ritual while holding it, including during SR. Cannot bind artifact/sentient weapon. Dismiss bound weapon into extradimensional space and summon instead of creating a new weapon. Bond ends on death, bonding another, or 1-hour severing ritual; weapon appears at feet if it was extradimensional. Does not grant CHA attacks or an intrinsic +1 bonus.

Chain: Luceat support: not verified. learn find familiar, not counted against spells known, and cast as ritual. Ordinary forms plus imp, pseudodragon, quasit, sprite. When taking Attack action, may forgo one attack so familiar makes one attack using its reaction. No default bonus-action familiar attack. Familiar uses full 2014 spell/stat-block rules; monster variant familiar traits do not automatically transfer to a summoned familiar.

Tome: Luceat support: not verified. select three cantrips from any classes, counted as warlock spells but not against base known-cantrip allowance; may cast while book is on your person. If lost, 1-hour ceremony during SR/LR replaces it, destroys old book. Book becomes ash on death.

Mirror Veil Patron

  • L1 Fey Presence: Luceat support: not verified. action; creatures in 10-ft cube originating from you make WIS saves or become charmed/frightened by you, your choice, until end of next turn. Once/SR or LR.
  • L6 Misty Escape: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when damaged; become invisible and teleport up to 60 ft to visible unoccupied space. Invisible until start of next turn or until attacking/casting spell. Once/SR or LR.
  • L10 Beguiling Defenses: Luceat support: not verified. immune to charmed. When creature attempts to charm you, reaction to force WIS save; failure charmed by you for 1 minute or until you/companions damage it.
  • L14 Dark Delirium: Luceat support: not verified. action, visible creature within 60 ft; WIS save or charmed/frightened by you for 1 minute with concentration, ends if damaged. It perceives only itself, you, and the illusory environment chosen for it. Not automatically incapacitated. Once/SR or LR.

The Fiend

  • L1 Dark One’s Blessing: Luceat support: not verified. when you reduce hostile creature to 0 HP, temporary HP = max(1, CHA + warlock L).
  • L6 Dark One’s Own Luck: Luceat support: not verified. add d10 to your ability check/save after initial roll but before effects; once/SR or LR.
  • L10 Fiendish Resilience: Luceat support: not verified. after SR/LR choose one damage type to resist until choosing again. Damage from magical weapons or silver weapons bypasses this resistance.
  • L14 Hurl Through Hell: Luceat support: not verified. on attack hit, transport target away until end of your next turn; returns to prior or nearest available space. Non-fiend takes 10d10 psychic on return. Once/LR. Original attack still resolves its own damage.

Deep Current Patron

  • L1 Awakened Mind: Luceat support: not verified. telepathically speak to visible creature within 30 ft; no shared language needed but recipient must understand a language. Does not automatically grant reciprocal telepathic speech.
  • L6 Entropic Ward: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when creature attacks you, impose disadvantage. If it misses, your next attack against it before end of your next turn has advantage. Once/SR or LR.
  • L10 Thought Shield: Luceat support: not verified. thoughts unreadable by telepathy/other means unless permitted; psychic resistance; whenever creature deals psychic damage to you, it takes same amount of damage you take.
  • L14 Create Thrall: Luceat support: not verified. action, touch incapacitated humanoid; charmed until remove curse, another charm-removing effect, or using this feature again. Telepathic communication on same plane. Charmed does not grant unrestricted control of actions.
