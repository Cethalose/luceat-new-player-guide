Shared spellcasting progression

Spellcasting slots recover on LR. Cantrips do not use slots. A spell must be known or prepared as required and cast using a slot at least equal to its level. Higher-level slots do not themselves authorize learning higher-level spells. Ritual casting adds 10 minutes, uses no slot, and cannot raise the spell’s level.

Domain/oath/land spells are always prepared, count as spells of the granting class, and do not reduce preparation capacity. Patron spells only expand the warlock’s choices; they are not automatically known. Magical Secrets spells count as bard spells. “Know” and “prepare” are not interchangeable states.

Fighter/Rogue one-third spellcasting

Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster start at L3. Both use INT and wizard spells. EK knows two wizard cantrips at L3 and three at L10; AT knows mage hand plus two other wizard cantrips at L3, plus one more at L10. Cantrips are separate from leveled spells known. At L3 each knows three L1 spells: two school-restricted and one unrestricted. EK restricted schools are abjuration/evocation; AT restricted schools are enchantment/illusion. Newly learned leveled spells are school-restricted except the spells learned at L8, L14, and L20. Each class level may replace one known spell with an eligible wizard spell; replacement preserves the school restriction except when replacing an unrestricted spell from L3/8/14/20. Neither subclass grants ritual casting or use of an arcane focus.

Shared fighting styles

Choose only from the class’s list: Fighter = all six; Paladin = Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection; Ranger = Archery, Defense, Dueling, Two-Weapon Fighting. The same style cannot be selected twice even if a feature grants another choice.

StyleMechanical effect
Archery+2 to attack rolls made with ranged weapons.
Defense+1 AC while wearing armor.
Dueling+2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon wielded in one hand while wielding no other weapon. A shield is not a weapon merely because it is held.
Great Weapon FightingWhen a damage die for an attack with a melee weapon wielded in two hands rolls 1 or 2, reroll that die and use the new result. Weapon must have two-handed or versatile property. Treat the weapon’s own damage dice as the affected dice; preserve an interpretation flag if the implementation uses a different adjudication.
ProtectionWhen a creature you can see attacks a target other than you within 5 ft of you, use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll. Requires wielding a shield.
Two-Weapon FightingAdd the relevant ability modifier to damage of the second attack granted by two-weapon fighting. It does not waive weapon eligibility or bonus-action cost.

Spellbook (L1): Luceat support: not verified. six L1 wizard spells initially; two eligible spells per additional wizard level. Finding spells permits additional copying: 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level, only spells of a level you can prepare. Copying from your own book costs 1 hour and 10 gp per level. If book lost, can copy currently prepared spells into replacement at latter rate; recover other spells by finding them again. Cantrips are fixed in mind, not copied into spellbook.

Arcane Recovery (L1): Luceat support: not verified. once per day when finishing SR, recover expended slots totaling ≤ ceil(L/2), none L6+. Preserve the PHB’s once-per-day wording; do not equate every short rest to a reset. Spell Mastery (L18): Luceat support: not verified. choose one L1 and one L2 wizard spell in spellbook; while prepared, cast at lowest level without slots. Higher-level casting still spends slots. Eight hours of study can replace either/both with same-level spells. Signature Spells (L20): Luceat support: not verified. choose two L3 wizard spells in book; always prepared without preparation-capacity cost. Each cast once at L3 without slot per SR/LR. Higher-level casts require slots. No general repeatable replacement stated.

School Savant (L2 in every school): Luceat support: not verified. halve time and gold to copy spells of that school into spellbook. Each school below includes this feature even when not repeated.

School of Warding

  • L2 Arcane Ward: Luceat support: not verified. when casting abjuration spell of L1+, may create ward simultaneously; maximum HP 2 × wizard L + INT, initially full; lasts until LR. When you take damage, ward takes it instead; excess reaches you. At zero HP it ceases absorbing but remains. Each later L1+ abjuration cast restores 2 × spell level ward HP to cap. Once created, cannot create another until LR. Ward is a separate pool, not temporary HP; resolve its damage before your resistance/temporary HP.
  • L6 Projected Ward: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when visible creature within 30 ft takes damage; ward absorbs that damage, excess reaches creature.
  • L10 Improved Abjuration: Luceat support: not verified. add PB to ability checks made as part of casting abjuration spells, including counterspell/dispel magic checks.
  • L14 Spell Resistance: Luceat support: not verified. advantage on saves against spells; resistance to damage from spells.

School of Binding Lines

  • L2 Minor Conjuration: Luceat support: not verified. action; create an inanimate object in hand or unoccupied ground within 10 ft, using the form of a nonmagical object you have seen. Maximum 3 ft on a side and 10 lb. The created object is visibly magical and emits dim light 5 ft. Vanishes after 1 hour, on next use, or if it takes/deals damage.
  • L6 Benign Transposition: Luceat support: not verified. action; teleport up to 30 ft to visible unoccupied space, or exchange positions with a willing Small/Medium creature in that range. Once, restored by LR or casting a L1+ conjuration spell.
  • L10 Focused Conjuration: Luceat support: not verified. taking damage cannot break concentration on a conjuration spell; other concentration-ending events still apply.
  • L14 Durable Summons: Luceat support: not verified. a creature summoned/created with a conjuration spell has 30 temporary HP.

School of Open Omens

  • L2 Portent: Luceat support: not verified. after LR roll two d20s and record. Replace an attack, save, or ability-check roll by you or visible creature with a recorded result, before the roll. At most once per turn; each result once. Unused results lost at next LR.
  • L6 Expert Divination: Luceat support: not verified. when casting L2+ divination using slot, regain one expended slot of a lower level, maximum L5.
  • L10 The Third Eye: Luceat support: not verified. action; choose one benefit until incapacitated or SR/LR: darkvision 60 ft; see Ethereal Plane within 60 ft; read any language; see invisible creatures/objects within 10 ft and line of sight. Once/SR or LR.
  • L14 Greater Portent: Luceat support: not verified. record three dice instead of two.

School of Silver Tongues

  • L2 Hypnotic Gaze: Luceat support: not verified. action, one visible creature within 5 ft that can see/hear you; WIS save or charmed, speed 0, incapacitated until end of next turn. Action each later turn extends through end of following turn. Ends if you move more than 5 ft away, target can neither see nor hear you, or target takes damage. After successful save or effect ends, cannot use on same creature until LR.
  • L6 Instinctive Charm: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when visible creature within 30 ft attacks you and another creature is within that attack’s range. Attacker WIS save; failure attacks closest creature other than you or itself; ties chosen by attacker. Decide before knowing attack result. Charm immunity protects. On successful save, cannot use on same attacker until LR.
  • L10 Split Enchantment: Luceat support: not verified. a L1+ enchantment spell targeting only one creature can target a second creature.
  • L14 Alter Memories: Luceat support: not verified. when casting enchantment to charm creatures, may prevent one creature from knowing it was charmed. Once before spell expires, action to make that creature forget some charmed time: INT save against wizard DC, failure loses up to 1 + CHA hours, minimum 1. Cannot erase time before spell began.

School of Evocation

  • L2 Sculpt Spells: Luceat support: not verified. when casting evocation affecting other visible creatures, choose 1 + spell level of them. They automatically succeed on saves and take no damage if ordinarily successful save would halve damage. Does not protect against damage with no qualifying saving throw.
  • L6 Potent Cantrip: Luceat support: not verified. creature successfully saving against your damaging cantrip takes half damage, if any, but suffers no additional effect.
  • L10 Empowered Evocation: Luceat support: not verified. add INT to one damage roll of a wizard evocation spell you cast.
  • L14 Overchannel: Luceat support: not verified. when casting L1–5 wizard spell dealing damage, may maximize its damage. First use safe; subsequent use before LR deals 2d12 necrotic per spell level immediately after casting, increasing by 1d12 per spell level on each further use. This self-damage ignores resistance/immunity. Cantrips ineligible.

School of Shifting Veils

  • L2 Improved Minor Illusion: Luceat support: not verified. learn minor illusion, or different wizard cantrip if already known; additional to cantrip allowance. Minor illusion can create both sound and image with one casting.
  • L6 Malleable Illusions: Luceat support: not verified. action to change an illusion spell you cast with duration ≥1 minute while you can see it; changes must stay within that spell’s normal parameters.
  • L10 Illusory Self: Luceat support: not verified. reaction when creature attacks you; that attack automatically misses. Once/SR or LR.
  • L14 Illusory Reality: Luceat support: not verified. when casting L1+ illusion, choose an inanimate nonmagical object in it to become real for 1 minute; may make that object real on your turn as bonus action while spell continues. Object cannot deal damage or directly harm anyone. Do not turn an illusory creature into a creature stat block.

School of Quiet Graves

  • L2 Grim Harvest: Luceat support: not verified. once per turn when killing one or more creatures with L1+ spell, heal 2 × spell level, or 3 × spell level for necromancy. No benefit for killing constructs/undead.
  • L6 Undead Thralls: Luceat support: not verified. add animate dead to spellbook if absent. When casting it, can target one extra corpse/pile of bones to create another zombie/skeleton as appropriate. Whenever creating undead with necromancy spell, HP maximum increases by wizard L and weapon damage rolls add your PB.
  • L10 Inured to Undeath: Luceat support: not verified. necrotic resistance; HP maximum cannot be reduced.
  • L14 Command Undead: Luceat support: not verified. action; visible undead within 60 ft makes CHA save against wizard DC. Success prevents using this feature on it again. Failure makes it friendly and obedient until you use feature again. INT score ≥8 gives advantage on save; INT ≥12 also repeats save at end of each hour until free.

School of Translation

  • L2 Minor Alchemy: Luceat support: not verified. alter one nonmagical object composed entirely of wood, stone (not gemstone), iron, copper, or silver into another listed material. Ten minutes work per cubic foot; transformation lasts 1 hour or until you lose concentration as for a spell. One-hour duration does not multiply by processed volume.
  • L6 Transmuter’s Stone: Luceat support: not verified. 8 hours to create; holder gains selected benefit: darkvision 60 ft; +10 ft speed while unencumbered; CON saving-throw proficiency; or resistance to one of acid/cold/fire/lightning/thunder. On casting L1+ transmutation, may change benefit if stone on your person. One stone at a time; making another ends earlier stone’s function.
  • L10 Shapechanger: Luceat support: not verified. add polymorph to book if absent. Once/SR or LR, cast without slot on yourself only, becoming beast CR ≤1. Can otherwise cast normally with slots/restrictions.
  • L14 Master Transmuter: Luceat support: not verified. action consumes stone’s magic for one effect below. Cannot create another until LR. Stone is destroyed.
