# Luceat 2014-compatible mechanical progression

This D&D ruleset supplement describes the intended level-by-level mechanics for Luceat's twelve represented 2014-compatible base classes and forty mapped paths. The explanations are independently written. Intended behavior is a specification, not a support claim.

> **Implementation boundary:** Every mechanic below is the intended progression. Until a feature carries an explicit **Available in Luceat** statement, treat it as **not yet verified as usable in ordinary Luceat play**. Existing character-choice cards may expose partial effects; partial exposure does not imply the complete feature is implemented.

## Shared spellcasting progression

Spellcasting slots recover on LR. Cantrips do not use slots. A spell must be known or prepared as required and cast using a slot at least equal to its level. Higher-level slots do not themselves authorize learning higher-level spells. Ritual casting adds 10 minutes, uses no slot, and cannot raise the spell’s level.

| Class | Ability | Selection | Preparation/replacement | Rituals / focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bard | CHA | Known spells per table | On gaining a bard level, may replace one bard spell with an eligible bard spell | Known bard ritual; musical instrument |
| Cleric | WIS | Prepare max(1, L + WIS) cleric spells | Change after LR; preparation requires at least 1 minute per spell level for each spell on new list | Prepared cleric ritual; holy symbol |
| Druid | WIS | Prepare max(1, L + WIS) druid spells | Same preparation timing as cleric | Prepared druid ritual; druidic focus |
| Paladin | CHA | From L2, prepare max(1, floor(L/2) + CHA) paladin spells | Change after LR; at least 1 minute per spell level for each prepared spell | No inherent ritual casting; holy symbol |
| Ranger | WIS | From L2, known spells per table | On gaining a ranger level, may replace one known ranger spell with eligible ranger spell | No inherent rituals or focus permission |
| Sorcerer | CHA | Known spells per table | On gaining a sorcerer level, may replace one known sorcerer spell with eligible sorcerer spell | No inherent rituals; arcane focus |
| Warlock | CHA | Known spells per table; Pact Magic slots are separate | On gaining a warlock level, may replace one known warlock spell with eligible warlock spell of levels 1–5 | No inherent rituals; arcane focus |
| Wizard | INT | Spellbook: six L1 spells initially; two eligible wizard spells added each wizard level | Prepare max(1, L + INT) from spellbook after LR; at least 1 minute per spell level on new list | Wizard ritual in spellbook, even unprepared; arcane focus |

Domain/oath/land spells are always prepared, count as spells of the granting class, and do not reduce preparation capacity. Patron spells only expand the warlock’s choices; they are not automatically known. Magical Secrets spells count as bard spells. “Know” and “prepare” are not interchangeable states.

### Fighter/Rogue one-third spellcasting

Eldritch Knight and Arcane Trickster start at L3. Both use INT and wizard spells. EK knows two wizard cantrips at L3 and three at L10; AT knows mage hand plus two other wizard cantrips at L3, plus one more at L10. Cantrips are separate from leveled spells known. At L3 each knows three L1 spells: two school-restricted and one unrestricted. EK restricted schools are abjuration/evocation; AT restricted schools are enchantment/illusion. Newly learned leveled spells are school-restricted except the spells learned at L8, L14, and L20. Each class level may replace one known spell with an eligible wizard spell; replacement preserves the school restriction except when replacing an unrestricted spell from L3/8/14/20. Neither subclass grants ritual casting or use of an arcane focus.

## Shared fighting styles

Choose only from the class’s list: Fighter = all six; Paladin = Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection; Ranger = Archery, Defense, Dueling, Two-Weapon Fighting. The same style cannot be selected twice even if a feature grants another choice.

| Style | Mechanical effect |
|---|---|
| Archery | +2 to attack rolls made with ranged weapons. |
| Defense | +1 AC while wearing armor. |
| Dueling | +2 to damage rolls with a melee weapon wielded in one hand while wielding no other weapon. A shield is not a weapon merely because it is held. |
| Great Weapon Fighting | When a damage die for an attack with a melee weapon wielded in two hands rolls 1 or 2, reroll that die and use the new result. Weapon must have two-handed or versatile property. Treat the weapon’s own damage dice as the affected dice; preserve an interpretation flag if the implementation uses a different adjudication. |
| Protection | When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you within 5 ft of you, use your reaction to impose disadvantage on that attack roll. Requires wielding a shield. |
| Two-Weapon Fighting | Add the relevant ability modifier to damage of the second attack granted by two-weapon fighting. It does not waive weapon eligibility or bonus-action cost. |

**Rage (L1).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Bonus action on your turn; lasts 1 minute. While raging and not in heavy armor: advantage on STR checks and saves; add the table’s Rage Damage to melee weapon damage using STR; resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Cannot cast spells or concentrate while raging. Ends if unconscious, if you end it with a bonus action, or at end of your turn when you have neither attacked a hostile creature since your last turn nor taken damage since then. Spend one use on entry; all uses recover on LR. L15 Persistent Rage removes the inactivity ending condition, but not the 1-minute duration. L20 has unlimited entries, not one indefinite rage.

**Unarmored Defense (L1).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Without armor, AC = 10 + DEX + CON; shield allowed. **Reckless Attack (L2).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Decide when making your first attack on your turn: gain advantage on STR-based melee weapon attacks that turn; attacks against you gain advantage until your next turn. **Danger Sense (L2).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Advantage on DEX saves against visible effects while not blinded, deafened, or incapacitated. **Extra Attack (L5).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Two attacks. **Fast Movement (L5).** *Luceat support: not verified.* +10 ft speed while not wearing heavy armor.

**Feral Instinct (L7).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Advantage on initiative. If surprised at combat’s start and not incapacitated, may act normally on your first turn only if you enter rage before doing anything else. **Brutal Critical (L9/13/17).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Add 1/2/3 additional weapon damage dice to a melee critical hit; these dice are in addition to the critical hit’s ordinary doubled dice. **Relentless Rage (L11).** *Luceat support: not verified.* When damage reduces you to 0 HP while raging without killing you outright, CON save DC 10; success leaves 1 HP. Each use after the first before SR/LR adds 5 to DC; SR/LR resets DC to 10. **Indomitable Might (L18).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Replace a STR check total below your STR score with that score. **Primal Champion (L20).** *Luceat support: not verified.* STR and CON scores +4, maximums 24.

### Path of the Berserker

- **L3 Frenzy:** *Luceat support: not verified.* choose when entering rage. On each subsequent turn in that rage, may make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action. Gain one exhaustion level when the rage ends. This does not grant the bonus attack on the rage-entry turn.
- **L6 Mindless Rage:** *Luceat support: not verified.* cannot become charmed or frightened while raging; suspend an existing charm/frightened effect during rage.
- **L10 Intimidating Presence:** *Luceat support: not verified.* action; choose one creature you can see within 30 ft that can see or hear you. WIS save DC `8 + PB + CHA`; failure frightened of you until end of your next turn. On following turns, use action to extend to end of next turn. Ends if target ends its turn out of your sight or more than 60 ft away. On a successful save, target is immune to your use of this feature for 24 hours.
- **L14 Retaliation:** *Luceat support: not verified.* when a creature within 5 ft damages you, reaction to make a melee weapon attack against it.

**Bardic Inspiration (L1).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Bonus action, another creature within 60 ft that can hear you. It receives one die, lasting 10 minutes. Once, add it to an ability check, attack roll, or save after rolling d20 but before knowing success/failure. It is then spent; a creature can hold only one Bardic Inspiration die at a time. You have max(1, CHA) uses; LR recovery, upgraded to SR/LR at L5. Die d6 → d8 at L5 → d10 at L10 → d12 at L15.

**Jack of All Trades (L2).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Add floor(PB/2) to ability checks not already including PB, including initiative. **Song of Rest (L2).** *Luceat support: not verified.* During SR, perform for listeners; you and friendly creatures hearing it that regain HP by spending at least one Hit Die gain one additional die of HP: d6; d8 L9; d10 L13; d12 L17. Once per qualifying rest, not per Hit Die. **Expertise (L3, L10).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Select two proficient skills at each milestone and double PB for those checks; bard Expertise does not offer thieves’ tools.

**Countercharm (L6).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Action to perform until end of next turn. You and friendly creatures within 30 ft that can hear gain advantage on saves against frightened/charmed. Ends early if incapacitated, silenced, or voluntarily ended without an action. Does not remove an existing condition by itself. **Magical Secrets (L10/14/18).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Two spells from any classes at each milestone, cantrips allowed, of levels you can cast using the bard table. Count as bard spells and against spells known. **Superior Inspiration (L20).** *Luceat support: not verified.* When rolling initiative with zero Inspiration uses, regain one.

### College of Lore

- **L3 Bonus Proficiencies:** *Luceat support: not verified.* any three skills.
- **L3 Cutting Words:** *Luceat support: not verified.* reaction when a creature you can see within 60 ft makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll. Expend/roll Inspiration and subtract from that roll. Decide after the roll but before the DM determines attack/check success or before damage is dealt. Cannot affect a creature unable to hear you or immune to charmed. This does not target saving throws.
- **L6 Additional Magical Secrets:** *Luceat support: not verified.* two spells using Magical Secrets eligibility. These do not count against bard spells known.
- **L14 Peerless Skill:** *Luceat support: not verified.* when making an ability check, expend/roll Inspiration and add to your total after d20 roll but before outcome. The use remains spent even if the check fails.

### College of Battle Hymns

- **L3 Combat Inspiration:** *Luceat support: not verified.* recipient may instead add its Inspiration die to a weapon damage roll it just made; or use reaction when attacked, roll/add to AC against that attack after seeing attack roll but before knowing whether it hits. Either spends the die.
- **L6 Extra Attack:** *Luceat support: not verified.* two attacks.
- **L14 Battle Magic:** *Luceat support: not verified.* after using your action to cast a bard spell, make one weapon attack as a bonus action.

**Channel Divinity (L2).** *Luceat support: not verified.* Shared pool for Turn Undead and domain options; one use, two at L6, three at L18. SR/LR recovery. DC = cleric spell DC. **Turn Undead:** action, present holy symbol; every undead within 30 ft that can see or hear you makes WIS save. Failure: turned 1 minute or until damaged. **Domain weapon/cantrip progression.** *Luceat support: not verified.* Divine Strike (Life, Nature, Tempest, Trickery, War): once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, add 1d8 of domain’s type, 2d8 at cleric L14. **Spellbook (L1):** *Luceat support: not verified.* six L1 wizard spells initially; two eligible spells per additional wizard level. Finding spells permits additional copying: 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level, only spells of a level you can prepare. Copying from your own book costs 1 hour and 10 gp per level. If book lost, can copy currently prepared spells into replacement at latter rate; recover other spells by finding them again. Cantrips are fixed in mind, not copied into spellbook.

**Arcane Recovery (L1):** *Luceat support: not verified.* once per day when finishing SR, recover expended slots totaling ≤ ceil(L/2), none L6+. Preserve the PHB’s once-per-day wording; do not equate every short rest to a reset. **Spell Mastery (L18):** *Luceat support: not verified.* choose one L1 and one L2 wizard spell in spellbook; while prepared, cast at lowest level without slots. Higher-level casting still spends slots. Eight hours of study can replace either/both with same-level spells. **Signature Spells (L20):** *Luceat support: not verified.* choose two L3 wizard spells in book; always prepared without preparation-capacity cost. Each cast once at L3 without slot per SR/LR. Higher-level casts require slots. No general repeatable replacement stated.

**School Savant (L2 in every school):** *Luceat support: not verified.* halve time and gold to copy spells of that school into spellbook. Each school below includes this feature even when not repeated.

### School of Warding

- **L2 Arcane Ward:** *Luceat support: not verified.* when casting abjuration spell of L1+, may create ward simultaneously; maximum HP `2 × wizard L + INT`, initially full; lasts until LR. When you take damage, ward takes it instead; excess reaches you. At zero HP it ceases absorbing but remains. Each later L1+ abjuration cast restores `2 × spell level` ward HP to cap. Once created, cannot create another until LR. Ward is a separate pool, not temporary HP; resolve its damage before your resistance/temporary HP.
- **L6 Projected Ward:** *Luceat support: not verified.* reaction when visible creature within 30 ft takes damage; ward absorbs that damage, excess reaches creature.

- **L14 Spell Resistance:** *Luceat support: not verified.* advantage on saves against spells; resistance to damage from spells.
